Where Love Quietly Lives
Love is the sunrise after endless rain,
The gentle hand that softens every pain.
It's finding warmth beneath the winter skies,
And seeing whole galaxies in someone's eyes.

It's whispered dreams beneath the silver moon,
A favorite song, a soft and timeless tune.
It's silent moments where no words are said,
Yet feeling understood in every breath.

Love is not always roses, gold, or art,
It's choosing someone with your whole heart.
It's staying close when life begins to fall,
And being someone's shelter through it all.

It's little things that no one else can see,
A simple text that says, "Come home to me."
It's laughter dancing freely in the air,
And knowing someone's always there to care.

Love grows in patience, kindness, and trust,
In promises that never turn to dust.
It bends like branches when the wild winds blow,
Yet finds its strength in roots that gently grow.

Sometimes love arrives without a sound,
Like fallen stars that softly touch the ground.
Sometimes it leaves, yet teaches hearts to feel,
That even broken things can learn to heal.

For love is more than time or fate's design,
It's two imperfect souls that intertwine.
And though the world may change and drift apart,
True love leaves foreverprints upon the heart.
